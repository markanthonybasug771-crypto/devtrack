About DevTrack

Learn more about the Devotional Tracker and its purpose for the CLG-Laoag Music Team.

Built with purpose
OUR PURPOSE

DevTrack is a digital devotional companion made for the CLG-Laoag Music Team.

It was created to give every member an easy and meaningful way to reflect on God’s Word, document their personal devotions, and stay encouraged as one community.

What is it for?

This system serves as a simple online space where Music Team members can write their daily devotions, keep track of their spiritual growth, and share prayer needs with the team.

Why was it created?

It was built to help the team stay consistent in their quiet time with God, especially during busy ministry seasons. Instead of relying on scattered notes or forgotten habits, this platform brings everything into one place.

When was it created?

The system was created as a practical solution for the Music Team’s devotional journey, designed to support everyday spiritual discipline and unity within the ministry.

Who is it for?

It is made specifically for the members of the CLG-Laoag Music Team, helping them grow closer to God, encourage one another, and remain spiritually connected in their service.

What is the Admin Panel for?

The Admin Panel allows authorized leaders to manage users, review devotional entries, monitor prayer requests, and help provide spiritual encouragement and accountability within the Music Team.

What can DevTrack do?

DevTrack provides devotional journaling, prayer request tracking, devotional review, user management, and other tools designed to support the spiritual growth of the Music Team.
